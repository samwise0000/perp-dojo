Connect a read-only Bybit key

  1. In Bybit, open Account → API → Create New Key and choose System-generated API Keys.
  2. Select Read-Only. Under Contract, tick Orders and Positions. Leave everything else off.
  3. Binding your IP is optional. Without it, Bybit expires the key after 90 days.
  4. Running locally: copy .env.example to .env, paste the key and secret, and restart with npm start.
  5. On Vercel: add BYBIT_API_KEY and BYBIT_API_SECRET in the project's Environment Variables and redeploy.

Only the server uses the key, never this page, and it refuses keys that can trade or withdraw.

Pull your trade history

Your key checks out as read-only. Pick the exact days to pull from Bybit. It keeps about two years of closed perp trades.

Using a new browser or computer? Load from server brings back the trades and journal you last saved.

                      Every swing failure signal

                      4h swing failures on your 63-pair watchlist, with the same rules and filters as the Telegram alerts. Closed 4h candles only. Each is scored as a trade: entry at the signal candle's close, stop at its wick, target at the opposing swing level the move started from, whichever is hit first (a candle that touches both counts as the stop). Results are in R, before and after Bybit's standard fees. Click a row to see the trade on the chart.

                      Scanning your watchlist

                      Trades

                      Click a trade to replay it and add notes or tags.

                      Each trade is one close, as Bybit reports it in Closed P&L (USDT/USDC perps and inverse contracts). Net P&L is Bybit's figure, after opening and closing fees and funding. Funding per trade is derived as price P&L − fees − net P&L. Inverse contracts are converted to USD at the exit price. Days and hours are in your local time zone.
